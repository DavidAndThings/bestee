FINANCIAL_METRIC ni NET_INCOME 2024 4
FINANCIAL_METRIC ebitda EBITDA 2024 4

FINANCIAL_METRIC equity TOTAL_EQUITY 2024 4
FINANCIAL_METRIC ltdebt LONG_TERM_DEBT 2024 4
COMPUTED_METRIC altman_proxy ((((ni) * 3.3) + ((rev) * 1.0)) / assets) + (equity / (assets + ltdebt))
COMPUTED_METRIC capital_ratio (equity - ltdebt) / ((assets + 1) * 0.5)
COMPUTED_METRIC ebitda_leverage (ebitda - (ltdebt * 0.05)) / ((assets - equity) + 1)
COMPUTED_METRIC composite_score ((altman_proxy * 10) + (capital_ratio * 100)) + (ebitda_leverage * 0.001)
